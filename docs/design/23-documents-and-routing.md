# Documents and routing

Version 2.7: the library, a document's page, templates, notes and wiki, route templates and the flow editor, and routing (decisions D.1–D.4). Cards: "Templates · Documents and routing". Library, Templates, Route templates and Notes and wiki stay separate sidebar items (D.1).

## Library

Issued documents, browsed through a rail of saved-search folders beside a list or grid (D.4): All documents, Created by me, Waiting for me, Approved by me, From my unit, Passed through my unit, then Starred and the person's own collections. The current folder is tinted, with its count. A search, Template and Unit filters and List or Grid sit above the table. Filing only adds; nothing is moved or deleted from the library.

## A document's page

Every issued document has a record page (D.3) with four tabs: **Document** (the rendered PDF with its key facts and versions), **Routing** (where it is and what you can do), **History** (in sentences) and **Verification** (the QR code, scans, and new or withdrawn codes). Download PDF and Print are visible buttons in the header; Withdraw this document is in ⋯ and asks first.

## Routing

The Routing tab replaces the separate Circulation page; its old address redirects here. Each route a document is on shows its stages top to bottom: done stages green with a check, the current stage in the main colour, the next ones grey, each with the people it reached (names, never ids), the rule and the time. When the document waits for you, **Your decision** sits beside the route: an optional note everyone on the route sees, then **Approve**, Reject, Return to the sender and Forward to someone else, and one sentence saying what happens next.

## Templates, notes and wiki

Templates are listed with their kind and record type in words, their status (Draft; Published, version N) and **who can see it** in a sentence. Notes and wiki pages use the same list, with New note and New wiki page both visible. Both open in the document editor (section 18).

## Route templates and the flow editor

Each route is described in a sentence ("Branch manager, then the owner over JOD 1,000, then Finance"). The flow editor draws it top to bottom (D.2): a start pill, stages as cards (who it reaches, decision or sent only, the rule, conditions), branches side by side under a decision with Approved and Rejected on their arrows, and end pills. The selected stage's settings are in an inspector beside it: name, who it goes to (with how many people it reaches today), what they do, what counts as approved, and Only when. Rename and **Save route** are in the header.

The 2.7 Arabic card is the routing tab (`TplDocRoutingAr`).
