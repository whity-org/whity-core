# Logos

Drawn from the locked numbers in the decision register (stage 1). One module m is the width of the blue edge; the mark is side 8 m, offset 3 m, radius 2 m, edge 1 m on a 12 m canvas. The wordmark is Readex Pro 600 at −1%, with a rounded-square dot on the i and outside corners rounded to ¼ of the stem. In the lockup the word's cap line and baseline are the platform's top and bottom; the gap and the clear space are 3 m.

- `-colour`: Whity Blue #0961bb, white window, ink word #0a0a0a. Light grounds.
- `-reversed`: #59a0f9, white window, word #f2f3f5. Dark grounds.
- `-onbrand`: all white, window knocked out. On Whity Blue only.
- `-ink` / `-white`: one colour, window knocked out. Single-colour print, photos, other colours.
- `whity-mark-*`: the master, from 24px up. `whity-mark-16-*`: the pixel-fitted cut for 16–19px (a 20px cut is in `handoff/logos`).
- `app-icon-1024.png`: Whity Blue tile, white mark at 61% (tile 18 m). Platforms apply their own corner mask.
- `built-on-whity-light.svg` / `-dark.svg`: the partner credit.

Minimum sizes: lockup 20px tall on screen, 6 mm in print; mark alone 16px. The favicon (16px cut, with its own dark-theme colour) and favicon.ico, PNG app icons (1024, 512, 192, 180) and the 20px cut are in `handoff/logos`.
