# Inbox (A02)

Work waiting for the person from every source that sends any: documents, leave, forms, and plugins. Agent proposals live in Approvals; the home merges both (H.2).

- **Tabs** per source, with counts, only when there's more than one source. **Waiting for me / Including done** is a segmented switch.
- **Table:** Item (icon tile, title, source), From (a person with avatar, or "Public form"), State, Arrived. Every row opens its item: a document opens its Circulation page, a form submission its record. Rows are oldest first.
- **States are words the app composes and translates**, not server sentences: Your decision, To review, For your information, You approved, You rejected, Returned to you. Fixes the inventory's untranslatable status phrases.
- **States (V.2):** loading rows; "Nothing is waiting for you" with a success mark; filtered empty; error with Try again.

Arabic: الوارد · بانتظاري · بما فيها المنجز · قرارك · للمراجعة · للعلم · وافقت.
