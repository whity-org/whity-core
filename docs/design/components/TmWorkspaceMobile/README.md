# Mobile: managing the workspace

On-the-go essentials (M.40); deep setup goes to the web.

- **Billing:** the plan in the app's main colour with the price and renewal date; people, agents and storage used; next invoice, payment method (last four digits), invoices. **Change plan** opens on the web.
- **Activity and security:** what needs attention first (people without two-step, with **Remind them**), then recent events: pauses, blocked sign-ins, role changes, agent work. The full audit log is on the web.
- **Workspace settings:** name, installation and colour at a glance; who can join, two-step for everyone (tier 3, asks for Face ID), email domain. Everything under **On the web** has the external-link icon.
- **Sending it to the web:** a sheet says why ("Roles are easier on a big screen") and offers **Email me the link** (it opens on the computer and the sign-in is approved on the phone, M.32) or **Open on this phone anyway**. The link works once, for 15 minutes.
