# Mobile: inventory

- **Stock by place:** one place at a time (tabs with how many are low); low numbers red; search or **scan** a barcode at the bottom.
- **An item:** stock in each place, its **batches** with expiry (the one to sell first in red), move some, see the recipe; **Count** and **Make a batch** in the bottom bar.
- **Making a batch:** how many (a stepper), every ingredient with what it needs and what's in stock; anything short is red with what to do (make fewer, or transfer first). Making it takes the ingredients out, adds the product with a batch number and expiry, and offers to print labels.
- **A transfer:** from and to, items with steppers, who takes it; the receiving place confirms on arrival.
