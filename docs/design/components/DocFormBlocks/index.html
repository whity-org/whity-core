Block sheet 8

What the print engine needs for 3.4

Everything the form printouts use that the engine can’t do today. The rest uses blocks that exist.

1. Section heading styles A–F
A heading variant from the theme (form.sectionStyle): B draws two rounded squares and the number as vector shapes; D a row of bars; E and F need a tint. Free workspaces are fixed to B.
2. Tints and boxes
A 10% tint of the print colour (E, F, code), a 1 pt bordered box with a radius (decision block, office strip, signature cards, record card).
3. Choice with every option
A choice answer that prints each option with ☒ / ☐, chosen only above 8 options (theme switch).
4. Collapsing and hiding
A section whose answers are all N/A prints as one line; an unanswered question prints “Not answered”; theme modes hide or print everything.
5. Running header and footer
Page 2 onward: the running header instead of the letterhead. Footer: reference, “Page x of y”, theme images (client marks) at the end side, optional QR on every page.
6. “continued”
A question whose answer breaks across a page repeats its number and question with “continued” at the top of the next page.
7. Word count
For questions with a limit: “142 of 300 words” under the answer.
8. Signature row
Name, role and the “Confirmed in Whity” line beside the drawn signature image (captured on a touch screen), on a line; a dashed line when none was drawn. Images in a row are not possible today.
9. Decision, approvals, office details
Blocks bound to the form’s approval route: the decision line, the decision block with QR, the approvals list with “Not counted”, and the office fields.
10. Letterhead layouts
Centred, and English with Arabic either side of the logo (letterhead.layout).
11. Two-column questions
A section-level switch: question at the start, answer at the end, as a borderless table with hairlines.
12. Plan gate
Form printout settings beyond the standard layout only on paid plans and trials; when a trial ends, new printouts use the standard layout, made ones never change.
