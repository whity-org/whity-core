# Waste and adjustments

**Waste and stock adjustments** (roadmap N5): every change to stock that isn't a sale, a delivery or a transfer.

- Each line: when, where, item, the change (losses red), a **reason** from a short list (end of day unsold, expired, damaged, count difference, other with a note), value at cost, who, and its state.
- **Approval above a value** (default JOD 25 a line, by the branch manager), through a flow; below it, it's done at once.
- **End-of-day waste** is a quick list on the phone (`TmOpsMobile`) with a reminder at closing time.
- Numbers: waste this week at cost and as a share of sales, what waits for approval, the most wasted item, what counts found.
