# Thermal receipt, 80 mm

The till receipt (roadmap N8) for 80 mm thermal printers, rendered by Whity Render beside the 13 documents (section 16).

- 72 mm printable width; Geist Mono for alignment; one weight for text, bold for the business name and total.
- Header: business name, branch and phone, tax number. Receipt number, date and time, cashier. Lines (quantity × item, amount), subtotal, **sales tax 16%**, **total in JOD to 3 decimals**, how it was paid.
- The **JoFotara QR** with "scan to verify", a thank-you line, and "Built on Whity" (optional, per plan).
- **Arabic:** the whole receipt mirrors; numbers stay Western digits, left to right; the business name is the tenant's own (here an Arabic shop name); "Built on Whity" stays in Latin letters.
- Printed by a phone or tablet over Bluetooth, or a till PC; also sent by WhatsApp as an image.
