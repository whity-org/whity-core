# Mobile: money

- **At a glance:** bank and cash, to collect, to pay, sales tax due; what waits for you; budgets that are over.
- **An expense claim:** the receipt, the amounts (tax worked out), shop, account and budget; when a budget is over, it says why it needs you. **Approve and refund**.
- **A card payment's receipt:** what was paid, where and on which card; **Take a photo** of the receipt or choose one; what it was for, a note. Receipts can also be forwarded by email.
- **Entries to check** (light accounting on the phone, X.1): the Bookkeeping assistant's sorted bank lines as dashed proposals with from → to accounts; matched ones can be posted together, guesses are amber and need a choice.
