# Subscriptions

Both kinds (SU.1): the workspace's **own Whity plan** stays under Billing (section 25); this page is for **plans a tenant sells to its customers** — memberships, weekly boxes, service plans.

- **Tabs:** Plans, Subscribers, Renewals, Settings. Numbers: active, monthly income, past due, paused.
- **Plans in tiers:** name, price and period in mono, what's included, how many subscribers, Edit. One tier can be marked "Chosen most" from real numbers, never by hand.
- **Past due:** who, which plan, what happened in plain words (card declined, expired, not enough in the account), and what's next.
- **When a payment fails:** retries (3 over 7 days by default), a WhatsApp each time with a link to pay, then the plan is paused, not cancelled. CliQ and card.
- Customers subscribe, skip, pause and change tier in the customer app (`TmSubscriptionsMobile`) or on the plans page.
