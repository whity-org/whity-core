# Receiving and bills

**Receiving goods and supplier bills** (roadmap N4).

- **Receiving:** against the purchase order, usually on the phone with the scanner (`TmOpsMobile`): what arrived per line, lots and expiry dates, shortages with a note, a photo of the delivery note. Stock goes up as it's received.
- **Three-way match:** each line ordered, received and billed side by side; differences in red, with a plain badge ("1 not received").
- **The supplier's bill:** read from the PDF or email by the Bookkeeping assistant; if it doesn't match, a warning says how and by how much, and the assistant proposes the fix (ask for a credit note). Or pay only what arrived. A matching bill goes straight to the payment flow.
