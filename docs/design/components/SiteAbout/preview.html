What we promise

A good company, and an honest one

We believe in open source, and we need to earn a living. These four promises are how we do both.

Open stays open

Whity Core stays open source for good. Nothing free today moves behind a paywall tomorrow.

Your data is yours

Export everything, any time, in open formats. Leaving costs nothing.

Free means free

The full product for up to 5 people, not a trial, no card. Passing a limit warns you first and never locks you out.

Plain pricing

Published prices, monthly for small companies, and no hidden fees.

A note from the founder

Every company I built a system for needed the same base, and every time I built it again from the start. Whity is that base, done properly once: open for anyone to read, in Arabic and English, and built for how companies actually work, down to their tax system, their payments and their currency.

If something in Whity doesn’t fit how your company works, tell us. That’s how it gets better.
