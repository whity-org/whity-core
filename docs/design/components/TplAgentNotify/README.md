# Agent notifications by email and WhatsApp

When something needs a person and they're not in the app (roadmap N9; push is `TmPush`). Like push (M.44), **these only open Whity**: no Approve link in an email or a WhatsApp message; decisions happen after signing in, with the facts in front of them.

- **Email:** from "[Tenant] via Whity", a subject that says what's asked, the tenant's logo, the proposal as a dashed card with the agent and who it's for, **Open in Whity**, how many more wait, and why they're getting it with where to turn it off.
- **WhatsApp** (from the tenant's business number): one short message in the person's language with two buttons, **Open in Whity** and **Not now**. Amounts can be hidden here too, following the lock-screen setting.
- Both follow Preferences › Notifications (which kinds, quiet hours) and are batched: one message per agent per hour at most, except sign-in approvals.
