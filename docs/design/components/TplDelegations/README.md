# Delegations (A14)

- **Table:** From → to (people with avatars, or a role badge), What (the permission in words), Where (unit or whole workspace), Until (a date, or No end date), Status. Fixes the inventory's "Role #3" and "OU #5".
- **Delegate** opens a drawer: to (person or role), what (from your own permissions only), where, until. Revoke from the row menu is tier 1 with Undo.
- Ended delegations stay listed under All, for the record.

Arabic: التفويضات · فوّض · من ← إلى · ماذا · أين · حتى · لا تاريخ انتهاء · منتهٍ.
