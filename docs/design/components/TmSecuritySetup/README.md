# Mobile: setting up security

- **Change password:** current and new in 52px fields, a strength meter, three checks (12 characters at least, not used before, not in known leaks), and **Sign out my other devices** on by default. The button sits in the bottom bar.
- **Authenticator app, on the same phone:** a QR can't be scanned by the phone showing it, so step 2 is **Add to an app on this phone** (opens the authenticator directly) or a key to copy, in mono with a copy button. Step 3 is the 6-digit code; **Turn on**.
- **Recovery codes:** ten codes in mono, used ones struck through, "8 of 10 left", **Copy** and **Save to Files**; **Make new codes** replaces them all. Showing the codes asks for Face ID first.
- **Passkeys:** each with where it lives, when it was added and last used; **This phone** marked; **Remove** on the others (asks for Face ID); **Add a passkey** at the bottom.
