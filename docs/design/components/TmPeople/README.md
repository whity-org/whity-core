# Mobile: people

Managing people on the go (M.40), for admins only.

- **People:** three tabs (Everyone, Want to join, Invited); rows with the picture, name, role, and a flag where it matters (no two-step, paused). Search at the bottom, with a **+** button beside it to invite, under the thumb.
- **A person:** picture, name, role and since when; Call, WhatsApp and Email as 48px round buttons; role, groups, unit and two-step (with **Remind**); **Pause access** (signs them out, deletes nothing), **Reset two-step** and **Remove** are tier 3 and show a lock: they ask for Face ID (M.8). Detailed permissions open on the web.
- **Want to join:** each request as a card: how they arrived, their email, the role they'll get (Member by default, changeable), **Decline** and **Let in**.
- **Invite people** (sheet): email or mobile number, a role as chips, **Send invitation**; or **Share a link** and **Show a QR** to scan in person.

On the web: roles and permissions, groups, units, delegations and relations (A11 to A15).
