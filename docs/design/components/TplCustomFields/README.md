# Custom fields

**Studio lite** (roadmap N2): a workspace adds fields to existing record types — contacts, items, tasks, appointments — without code.

- **Fields on a record type:** built-in fields (marked) and the workspace's own, with their kind, who added them and where they're used.
- **Adding a field:** the kinds as tiles (short or long text, choose one or several, amount, date, time range, a person, a contact, yes or no, file, formula), a name, choices, whether it shows in the list, whether it's required, and who can change it.
- A new field is usable at once: in lists and filters, as a document merge field (`{{contact.preferred_branch}}`), in flow conditions, and on the phone.
- Full custom record types (new kinds of records, relations, views) are Studio, in the long-term roadmap (L1).
