# Mobile home

Whity mobile is a do-on-the-go companion (M.1), drawn at 390 × 844 with 17px text and 48px targets (M.5).

- **Home** opens on **Waiting for you, one decision at a time** (M.10): a card with the actor (agent "for you", or the person who sent it), how long it has waited, the title, the amount in mono, why, and **Decline** and **Approve** in the lower half of the card. Agent proposals are dashed, as on the web. After each decision the next card comes up; "1 of 5 · oldest first" under it. **See as list** switches to the list; the choice is remembered.
- **List option:** rows of 64px with the icon tile, title, who, amount and age. Tapping a row opens the full decision.
- **A full decision:** a back button and "1 of 5" at the top, the actor chip, title, the facts (invoice, amount, overdue, how it's sent, how long Undo lasts), the message itself, and the **bottom action bar** (M.4): Edit, Decline, and **Approve**, the one main button, full width, in thumb reach.
- **Tab bar** (M.2): Home, Inbox (with its count), the centre **Create** button, Agents, More.
- **Thumb zone:** the lower 46% of the screen is easy to reach with one hand. Decisions, the main button, search (M.7) and navigation live there; the top is for reading.

Swipes do safe things by default; swipe to approve is a personal option, limited to tier 1 and 2 (M.6, M.9).
