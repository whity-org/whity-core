# Mobile: appointments

- **A staff day:** the week as a strip, the day as a timeline: booked (blue), checked in (green), requests (dashed amber, "needs your OK"), free time (dashed). Tapping a request opens Accept or Suggest another time.
- **An appointment:** the customer with Call and WhatsApp, the facts and note, **Move to another time**, **Cancel**, and **No-show** or **Check in** in the bottom bar.
- **Your availability,** set on the phone (X.1): hours per day per branch with switches, breaks, and which services you do.
- **A customer booking,** in the tenant's brand (the customer app or the booking page): pick a day from the strip and a time from a grid of 48px slots (taken ones struck through); **Book 10:15**.
