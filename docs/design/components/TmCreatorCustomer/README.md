# Mobile: creators, customers and sign-in approval

**Tenant creators** (M.30), from "Create a workspace":
1. **Where should it live?** Whity (app.whity.dev, free trial) or another installation by its address.
2. **Make it yours:** company name, a logo from the camera or photos, a main colour from swatches (or a custom one), with **Your app, live** previewing the result in that colour as they choose. Then invite people (contacts, link or QR), and plan and trial if the installation sells plans. The rest is emailed as a link to finish on the web.

**A tenant's customer** (M.31) arrives from the tenant's link, website or a QR on a receipt. The app opens already in the tenant's brand with the tenant's product name ("Example Bakery orders") and one line on what it's for. **Continue with Apple** or **Google** creates a Whity ID and joins them in one step; email is the fallback. A small "Built on Whity" names the platform. No story or tour unless they ask.

**Approving a sign-in elsewhere** (M.32): a sheet slides up with "Is this you signing in?", the browser, place and time, and three numbers; the person taps the one on the other screen, then Face ID. **No, it wasn't me** blocks the attempt and offers to change the password.
