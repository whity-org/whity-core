# Mobile: managing agents

For admins (M.40); talking to agents is the Agents tab (M.12).

- **Agents:** each with who it works for, what it's doing, and a status: Working, Needs you, Paused. **Pause all agents** is the emergency stop: they stop at once and their proposals wait.
- **An agent:** what it did today and what waits for you; **What it may do** in plain words with "On its own", "Asks you" or "Not allowed"; **Chat** and **Pause agent** in the bottom bar. Changing its permissions happens on the web.
- **Pausing** is easy and safe, so it needs no Face ID: one sheet says what happens (it stops now, its proposals wait, nothing is undone), **Pause now**, Cancel.
- **Activity:** today or this week, newest first; approved work with **Undo** and the time left; failures in red with the fix as a button.
