# Appointments

Customers and staff both book (AP.1). Staff manage the calendar here; customers book on the booking page (`TplBookingPage`) or in the customer app.

- **Day or Week**, by branch. Columns are **people and rooms**; each appointment shows the customer and the service. Booked is blue, **checked in** green, a **request** dashed amber ("needs your OK"), blocked time grey. A red line marks now.
- **Requests:** times a customer asked for outside instant booking (or when a service needs approval), with **Accept** or **Suggest another time**.
- **Today:** booked, checked in, requests, and the reminders sent (WhatsApp 24 hours and 2 hours before, by default).
- **Services:** name, length and price; each service names who can do it and which room it needs.
- Availability (working hours, breaks, time off) is set per person; meetings and committee meetings that book a room block it here too.
