# Mobile: the organisation

**Org drill-down plus "I'm away"** (OR.1):

- **Level by level:** where you are (Example Bakery › Branches), the unit, its manager, the units inside it and its people. Tap down; back goes up.
- **Changing a role:** roles as choices, each saying in plain words what it lets the person do; more powerful roles ask for Face ID. The full permissions grid stays on the web.
- **I'm away:** dates, who decides for you, what goes to them; anything above what they may approve waits for you. **Hand over from Tuesday**.
- **Delegations:** who decides for whom, what, when, and whether it's starting, in force or ended.
