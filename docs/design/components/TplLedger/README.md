# Accounts and sales tax

Light accounting (MO.1), in plain words with the account numbers beside them.

- **Accounts:** the chart of accounts as a tree (assets, liabilities, income, costs) with balances in mono. Names are plain ("Customers owe us", "Sales tax we can reclaim"); the codes are for the accountant.
- **A journal entry:** account, note, debit, credit, and **Balanced** at the foot; where it came from (an expense claim, an invoice, a bank line), who proposed the accounts (usually the **Bookkeeping assistant**) and who posted it. Entries from claims, invoices and payments are made for you; manual entries are for adjustments.
- **Sales tax return** for the period (two months in Jordan): tax on sales, tax on purchases, what to pay, the due date, and how many invoices were reported to **JoFotara**. **Download the return** to file with the Income and Sales Tax Department, then **Mark as filed**.
- Whity isn't the books of record for companies that keep an accountant's system: **Export for your accountant** hands over the entries and receipts.
