# Billing (A42)

- **Your plan:** plan and price (`[plan name]`, `[price]` until decided), renewal date, the plugins billed with it (S.2), and how the workspace pays.
- **Next payment:** the amount in JOD with 3 decimals, what it covers, and **Pay now** (the main button): methods, then the provider's page or bank-transfer details with a reference. **Change plan** opens the plans to compare (the built page said to contact support).
- **Invoices:** number, status, total, still to pay, due date; a row opens the invoice (DocPreview) with Download PDF and Pay.
- People without permission to pay see everything but Pay now, with who can pay.

Arabic card: `TplBillingAr`.
