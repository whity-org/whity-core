# A formal meeting

A meeting's page after it ends (MT.1). Facts in the header: when, where, whether the quorum was met, and whether it was **recorded with everyone told**, and for how long the recording is kept (30 days by default).

- **Tabs:** Agenda, Attendance (present, apologies, absent, guests), Minutes, Resolutions, Action items, Files.
- **Minutes:** the Minutes assistant drafts them from the recording and the secretary's notes, for the secretary, as a dashed draft. Item by item, in plain sentences, with resolution numbers. The secretary can **Edit**, **Send back to the assistant** with a note, or **Approve minutes**; the chair then signs, and the minutes are filed in Documents.
- **Resolutions** are numbered by meeting (14/1) with the votes for, against and abstained.
- **Action items** have an owner (a person or an agent), a due date, and become **Taskaat tasks** when the minutes are approved; an agent's item gets a done-when check from the discussion.
- Recording needs everyone told at the start (the app shows a banner to all joiners); without consent, minutes come from notes only.
