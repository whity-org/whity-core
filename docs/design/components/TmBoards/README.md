# Mobile: boards

- **One column at a time:** the columns as a row of pills at the top with counts (Review carries the gate flag); swipe sideways to move between them. Cards are 18px-cornered with the title, label, check progress and assignee. **Add task** in the bottom bar.
- **Moving a card:** press and hold; the card lifts onto the plane and a **Move to** sheet lists the columns, each saying what moving there means (Done skips review and marks it unverified). Dragging between columns at the screen edge also works.
- **Your boards** with what's open, who's on it, and how many wait for review; **New board**.
- **Board settings** on the phone (X.1): name, columns (drag to reorder; Awaiting review is locked while agents work there), who sees it, and whether agents can take tasks.
