# Meetings

Everyday meetings and formal ones in one list (X.3). Formal meetings belong to a **meeting body**: a committee with a chair, a secretary, members, a schedule, a quorum, and numbered meetings with minutes and resolutions.

- **Upcoming, Past, Mine**, filtered by body. Grouped by day; each row has the time and length, the title, where (room, video or both), what's ready (agenda, notes), and **Join** for video. Formal meetings have a blue edge and a Formal badge.
- Beside the list: what waits for the person (minutes to approve), and the **meeting bodies** with their chair, secretary, size, schedule and quorum.
- Everyday meetings: an agenda is optional, notes are taken by the Notes assistant if asked, and action items become tasks.
- Rooms and customer meetings come from Appointments, so a booked tasting appears here for the people in it.
