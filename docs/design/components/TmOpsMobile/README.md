# Mobile: contacts, receiving and waste

- **Contacts** (N1): all, customers, suppliers; balances in mono (owed to us red, owed by us with a minus); search with **+** beside it.
- **A contact:** WhatsApp, Call, Invoice; owes us and this year; the workspace's own fields (N2).
- **Receiving a delivery** (N4): against the purchase order, a stepper per line (a short line turns red), lot and expiry, a note, a photo of the delivery note; **Scan** or **Received**.
- **End-of-day waste** (N5): what's left unsold, a stepper per item, the value at cost, and who approves above the limit; reminded at closing time.
