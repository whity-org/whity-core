# Mobile: the customer app

What a tenant's customer sees after joining in one tap (M.31). The whole app wears the tenant's brand; the tenant's plugins decide the tabs and pages (here Example Bakery's orders plugin), and Whity supplies the shell, sign-in, payments and notifications.

- **Home:** what's due to pay as a card in the tenant's colour with **Pay now**; their orders with status; **Order the tray again**.
- **Paying an invoice:** the facts, then **CliQ** (default in Jordan), card, or cash on delivery, and one button with the amount.
- **Tracking an order:** when it arrives, four steps, the driver and address, call or message, and "Something's wrong with my order".
- **Account:** picture (a pattern in the tenant's colours, M.37), addresses, payment methods, notifications, language; the Whity ID, which also works in other apps built on Whity; sign out; "Built on Whity" at the foot.

Customers never see Manage, agents' approvals or the story; their tab bar is the plugin's (Home, Orders, New order, Messages, Account).
