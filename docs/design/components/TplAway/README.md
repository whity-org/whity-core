# I'm away, and delegations

**Org drill-down plus "I'm away"** (OR.1). "I'm away" is the delegation everyone needs; the full delegations list is beside it, in names (the built app shows "Role #3").

- **I'm away:** dates, **who decides for you** (with what they can already approve), and what goes to them: approvals in flows, your agents' proposals, documents to sign. Anything above what they may approve **waits for you**. A message for people who write to you. **Hand over from 10 November**.
- While it's on, flows route to the delegate (`TplFlowBuilder`), the delegate's Inbox says "for Amro", and everything they decide is recorded as theirs, on your behalf.
- **Delegations:** from, to (a person or a role), what, when, and whether it's starting, in force or ended. Coming back early ends it at once.
