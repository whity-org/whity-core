# Mobile: approvals in bulk and approval gating

- **Approving ordinary items together** (AQ.1): from Home's list option or the Inbox, press and hold to select; only tier-1 items get a tick box, others show a lock and open on their own. **Approve 4** in the bottom bar; each keeps its Undo.
- **Gating requests** (on the phone, X.1): Sign-ups, Passwords, Two-step, with who, why and how many approvals it has.
- **A two-step reset** (AG.1): what it does, where it came from, the checks done, and the two admins: the first with how they verified, you waiting. No Undo, said once.
- **Verifying as the second admin:** Face ID (or a passkey), then it's done and Ibrahim is told by email.
