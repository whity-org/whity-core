# Connect an AI app

The wizard on Connected AI apps, three steps with a Stepper:

1. **App:** large tiles (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, n8n, Other); one is chosen at a time.
2. **What it may do:** three choices in words: everything I can do, asking first for changes (the default); only look things up; choose area by area (opens the ScopeGrant list).
3. **Set up:** numbered steps for that app in its own words, the address to paste with **Copy address**, and a dashed "Waiting for … to connect" line. The dialog closes by itself when the app signs in and shows "Claude is connected". There's no main filled button on this step: the next move happens in the other app.

Apps that can't connect yet say so on their tile ("Coming soon").
