# Route flow editor (A32)

The stages a document travels, drawn **top to bottom** (D.2), so the route reads like a sentence.

- **Start and end** are pills ("A purchase order is sent", "Done", "Ends here"). **Stages** are cards: number and name, who it reaches in words, and tags (Decision or Sent, not asked; Any one decides or Everyone; conditions like Only over JOD 1,000). The selected stage has the primary ring.
- **Branches:** after a decision, Approved and Rejected continue side by side, each labelled on its arrow. Loops ("back to stage 1") are a labelled arrow, not a line across the page.
- **Add a stage** under the last stage, or between two (hover the arrow).
- **Inspector:** name, who it goes to (a person, role, group or the sender's manager, with "Reaches N people today"), what they do, what counts as approved, and Only when (conditions). Remove this stage.
- **Header:** Rename and **Save route** (the main button), both visible (fixes the inventory: the title couldn't be renamed). Leaving unsaved asks first (P.3).
- Warnings show on the stage they concern (a stage that reaches nobody, two paths that meet).

Arabic: محرر المسار · أضف مرحلة · تمت الموافقة · مرفوض · ينتهي هنا · لمن يذهب · ماذا يفعلون · فقط عندما · حفظ المسار.
