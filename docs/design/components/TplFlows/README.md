# Flows

**Workflows across everything** (FL.1): when something happens (a trigger), steps follow where **people decide** and **agents act**, with conditions and waits. A document's route (section 23) is one kind of flow; so are a form's approvals, spending limits, stock reorders and onboarding.

- Each flow is listed with **its sentence**: "When a purchase order is created over JOD 500, Emad then Amro approve it." The highlighted words are the parts you can change.
- Grouped by what they're about (Documents, Forms, Money, Stock, People), with how often each ran this month and whether it's On or a Draft.
- **Templates** to start from, and **Describe a flow**: say it in words and an agent drafts the steps for you to check.
- Route templates now live here (the sidebar's Work › Flows); a document still shows its route on its Routing tab.
