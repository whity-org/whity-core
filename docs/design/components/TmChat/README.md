# Mobile: chat

The fourth tab is **Chat** (CH.3), shared by people and agents (CH.1).

- **Chat tab:** groups and direct chats with people and agents mixed (All, People, Agents); agents carry the robot face and "Agent". Search at the bottom, **new chat** beside it.
- **A group with agents:** people on the left in grey bubbles, you on the right in blue, agents in a blue-tinted bubble with the **Agent** tag and who they work for. A system line records who added an agent and when. Proposals arrive as dashed cards with **Review and approve** for the person who must decide; "is drafting…" shows what an agent is doing. The composer: type with @ for people and agents, or hold to speak.
- **Adding an agent:** a sheet with People or Agents, the agent, what it can read (from now on by default), and who it works for here.
- **Chat details:** people, agents, and what each agent may do here.

Agents today (the day's agent activity) is the second view of the Chat tab (`TmCreateAgents`).
