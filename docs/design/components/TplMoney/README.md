# Money

Spending, getting paid and light accounting (MO.1). Tabs: Overview, Expenses, Invoices, Payments, Budgets, Accounts, Sales tax.

- **Overview:** what's in the bank and in cash, what's to collect (with how many are late), what's to pay, and the sales tax due with its period and date. A chart of money in less money out by month.
- **Waiting for you:** expense claims (receipts read by the Expense assistant), card payments missing a receipt (remind the cardholder), the **Bookkeeping assistant**'s sorted bank lines, and late invoices with the Billing assistant's proposed reminder.
- **Budgets** per team for the month, with what's spent; over budget is red and says what changes (here, new fuel claims need the owner's approval).
- **Export for your accountant** sends the period's entries, receipts and the sales tax summary in the format their system takes.
