Agents

Agents do the work. You stay in charge.

Each agent works for a person, can only do what that person allows, and shows everything it does.

Billing assistant · for AmroSend payment reminders to 12 customersJOD 4,860.000

The Billing assistant wants to send 12 payment reminders, JOD 4,860.000 in total. Try it: approve, then undo.

  1. 01

    An agent prepares the work

    A reminder, an order, a reply. It stays a dashed draft, marked as the agent’s, until someone decides.

  2. 02

    You decide

    Approve with one tap. Anything sensitive asks for Face ID, and nothing moves without you.

  3. 03

    It happens, with Undo

    Done in the open: who asked, who approved, and an Undo while it still can be undone.

Agents never

  • Do more than you allow. You choose, action by action: off, ask first, or allowed.
  • Have more rights than the person they work for.
  • Hide what they did. Every step is in the activity, with who it was for.
  • Pass for a person. Agents always show the robot face and are named by their job.

Questions

Straight answers

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Free for up to 5 people and 500 customers a month, with no card. It’s the full product, not a trial.

What happens when we grow past 5 people?

Whity tells you first. Nothing is locked or deleted; you choose a plan when you’re ready.

What can agents do without me?

Only what you allow, action by action. Anything set to “Ask first” waits for you.

Where is our data kept?

In the EU: our servers, database, files and backups are all stored there. If it must stay in your own country, we can host it there for you for a fee, or you run it on your own server.

Can we leave?

Any time. Export everything in open formats; leaving costs nothing.

Is it really in Arabic?

Every screen, document and message, written by people who speak it, not machine-translated.

Do I need a developer?

Not on Whity Cloud. To run it on your own server, yes, or we can set it up with you.

Who approves what?

You decide by role and amount; someone covers when you’re away, and every decision is recorded.
