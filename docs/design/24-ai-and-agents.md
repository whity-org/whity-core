# AI and agents

Version 2.8: Whity's own agents, outside AI apps connected to Whity, and what AI can do (decisions AI.1–AI.4). Cards: "Templates · AI and agents". The sidebar's AI group reads **Agents**, **Connected AI apps** and **What AI can do** (AI.1).

## Agents

An agent works for one person and never has more access than that person. The Agents page lists My agents and, for admins, Everyone's agents: the agent with its job in a line, who it works for, how much it does on its own (how many actions are Allowed and how many Ask first), when it was last active, and whether it's working or paused.

**New agent** opens a gallery of ready agents (AI.2): Billing assistant, Stock assistant, HR helper, Document drafter, Customer replies and more, each with what it's for, three things it does and what it needs. Start from blank is the last card. A new agent starts with every action on Ask first.

## An agent's page

A record page (AI.3) with tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What it does (including what it never does), today's activity, how much it does on its own, where it works (apps, channels, languages) |
| Instructions | Plain-language instructions written as to a new colleague; the pages and templates it may read; tone and language |
| What it may do | Off, Ask first or Allowed for each action, in plain words; actions the person lacks are shown with the reason |
| Activity | Everything it did and proposed, with Undo while the window is open |

See activity and Pause are visible buttons; Delete agent is in ⋯ and asks first.

## Connected AI apps

Outside apps (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, n8n and others) signed in to Whity, each for one person, with what it may do in words, when it was last used and when it expires. **Connect an AI app** is a three-step wizard: pick the app, choose what it may do (everything I can do asking first for changes, only look things up, or area by area), then follow the app's own setup steps while Whity waits for it to connect.

## What AI can do

The former MCP tools page lists every action AI can take in plain words, grouped by area, with how agents may use it (can do it; proposes and a person approves; suggests only; never) and what happens if it goes wrong (how long Undo lasts). Tool names, toolsets, discovery modes and token counts appear only with **Show technical details** (A.4, A.3).

The 2.8 Arabic card is an agent's page (`TplAgentRecordAr`).
