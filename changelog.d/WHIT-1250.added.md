- **Plugin notices by email.** A plugin's notice can now reach people by email as well as in the inbox, following each person's notification preferences and the workspace's email switch like core's own notices. A plugin opts in per type with `emailDefault` (SDK 1.135); people turn each type on or off by email in Preferences › Notifications, and their choice always wins. The email is in the person's language with the workspace's brand and carries only the notice's subject and body. Plugins that don't opt in behave exactly as before (WHIT-1250, #2209).
