- **Online booking: the widget, the cancellation policy and deposits.** A workspace can put a Book button on its own website with one script tag (Settings › Calendar › Your website): it opens the booking page over the site, and only the https sites the workspace lists may show it (none by default). The booking page says before Book until when cancelling or moving is free, and the manage page says it with the exact time; inside that window a customer can still move once online, and it doesn't count as a miss. Each service can ask for a deposit (none, a fixed amount or a percentage of the price) in the booking's currency. Online bookings pay it through the payment provider; without one connected, they are requests the business approves. It is kept after a late cancel or if they don't come, counts toward the bill when they come, and is refunded only when someone with `calendar:manage` presses Refund, which is written to the audit log. "Ask for a deposit" (off by default) asks it of customers who missed often. SDK 1.177: `BookingQuote::$depositMinor` and the optional `Calendar\BookingDepositRefunds`. New routes: `POST /api/v1/calendar/bookings/{id}/deposit/refund` (an MCP tool) and the public `GET /api/v1/public/booking/{workspace}/embed` (WHIT-1445).
