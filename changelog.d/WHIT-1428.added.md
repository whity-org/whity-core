- **Invite a customer to their account.** A contact's page has Invite to their account: the contact gets an email in the workspace's brand and language, and opening it (with a password, an existing account or a sign-in provider) gives them their account in the customer area, linked to that contact so their bookings and bills are there at once. It works even when public sign-up is off; the workspace chooses the role customers get in Settings › Sign-up. Booking, quote and message emails to someone who already has an account now also carry "See it in your account" (WHIT-1428).
