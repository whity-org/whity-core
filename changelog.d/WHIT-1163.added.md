- **A stage can ask for one of a list.** Besides a line of text and a person,
  a value a stage asks for can now be a choice with 2 to 12 options in English
  and Arabic, such as a faculty recommendation or the IRB's decision (Approved,
  Conditionally approved, Revisions required, Not approved). The person
  approving picks it from a list, only those options are accepted, and
  documents, printouts and the route page show the option's label (WHIT-1163).
