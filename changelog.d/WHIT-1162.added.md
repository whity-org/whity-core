- **A form's table can have a Person column, and the people in it are left out
  of approvals that exclude them.** An ethics form lists students and other
  researchers as rows of people, and anyone named there must not review the
  application. A table column can now be a person, picked like a person
  question and checked against the workspace. A route step that leaves out the
  people named in a table leaves out everyone in its person cells, and the
  printout lists them as "Not counted". Exports, printouts and final documents
  show each person by name. A table with a person column is not offered on a
  public form, like a person question (WHIT-1162).
