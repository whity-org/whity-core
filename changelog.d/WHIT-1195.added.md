- **New workspaces start from a template.** Right after a workspace is created (Whity ID sign-up, the plain sign-up, or Create workspace while signed in), its owner picks how they will use Whity: a ready template such as a salon, a clinic or a bakery, or Start blank. Each card says what it sets up; choosing one sets the basics up in a minute, free, with example customers and bookings if wanted. Its guided setup then opens in the usual setup stepper (business, hours, services, team, booking rules, the booking link), and when the template comes with a paid plugin, the last step says plainly what is free in Whity and what the plugin adds, with a one-click trial and no card. The template's remaining steps show under Finish setting up on the dashboard. Workspaces older than two weeks are never sent to the picker.
