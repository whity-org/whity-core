- **Your phone number, on your own account.** My settings › Profile has a Phone number field with a country picker (Jordan first, the region, the UK and the US, or any other country by its code), stored as E.164 and yours in every workspace (`GET`/`PUT /api/v1/me/phone`, migration 304). Booking pages now fill it in for you along with your name and email, and `GET /api/v1/me` returns it. A sign-in through the server's own identity provider that carries a verified `phone_number` fills an empty number, but never replaces or refills one you set or removed yourself; a workspace's own provider never sets it (WHIT-1181).
