- **A template's paid plugin waits on the dashboard as a locked card.** A workspace that started from a template whose offer names a paid plugin sees that plugin first under From your apps, dashed and locked, while the plugin is installed but the workspace has no plan and hasn't had its trial. The card names the plugin as people know it, says in two or three lines what it adds, and has one button: Start your 30-day free trial. See what's included opens the plugin on Settings › Plugins. Once the trial starts the card goes and the plugin's own cards take over. It hides from its ⋯ menu like any card, and only people who can turn plugins on see it.
