- **Plugins can be sold per seat.** A paid plugin can be priced per unit the workspace has: Whity for Salons, for example, is priced per chair. Settings › Plugins shows "3 chairs × JOD 15.000 = JOD 45.000 a month", and **Buy** checks out for that many. When the salon changes its number of chairs, the subscription follows, prorated by the payment provider. A provider that can't sell a quantity, such as Polar, is refused with a plain message rather than charged for one (SDK 1.110, WHIT-1152).
