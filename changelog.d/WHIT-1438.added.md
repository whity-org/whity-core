- **Choose which plugin screens your customers see.** Settings › Customer area now lists every enabled plugin's screens for customers, each with a switch, and says whether your customers can open it. When they can't yet, Give your customers this screen asks first, then adds it to your customer sign-up role and your existing customers' roles. Roles your team also uses, and roles shared by every workspace, stay as they are and are named in the question. A screen you turn off shows to no customer (WHIT-1438).
