- **Plugins can keep their customers and suppliers as core contacts (WHIT-1209, #2131).** SDK 1.119 adds the `Whity\Sdk\Contacts\Contacts` host service: a plugin lists, reads, creates and updates contacts, and finds one by its own field (`findByField($tenantId, 'keyhub.supplier_code', 'DK-0042')`), with no HTTP call and no session. Writes act as a person in the workspace who holds `contacts:write`, with the same checks as the contact routes. `PluginContactFieldsInterface` lets a plugin add its own fields to contacts, named under the plugin (`keyhub.supplier_code`). They are made in each workspace that turns the plugin on and archived, with their values kept, when it is turned off. They show on the contact screens marked "From <plugin>" and are offered in import column matching, and they don't count toward a workspace's 50 fields. People can't archive a plugin's field (409). `GET /api/v1/custom-fields` returns `owner_plugin`. Migration 306. English and Arabic.
