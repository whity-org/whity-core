- **Billing: plans are cards that say what they include, with a Monthly / Yearly switch.** Choosing or changing a plan now shows one card per plan with its price, what it comes with (staff, customers, storage, messages, agent runs and the rest, or "unlimited") and its button; a switch above the cards moves between monthly and yearly prices and says what yearly saves. The plans list (`GET /api/v1/billing/plans`) now returns each plan's entitlements.
