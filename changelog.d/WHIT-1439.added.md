- **Rooms and equipment.** Rooms become Rooms and equipment: each is a room, chair, piece of equipment or other, can belong to a group ("Chairs") and a branch. A service can need several, each a specific one or any free one from a group, so three chairs in one group take three bookings at once and a fourth is refused; the customer never chooses a chair. Moving a booking keeps what it held when that is still free, and the desk can pick another from the same group on the booking. The database refuses two bookings of the same chair or X-ray at once, whoever writes them. `calendar_rooms` is now `calendar_resources` (ids, bookings and services carry over); services and the API accept `resources` and still take `room_id` from older clients (WHIT-1439).
