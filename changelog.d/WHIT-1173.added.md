- **One booking can hold several services back to back, such as a cut, a colour and a blow-dry.** Turn on "Several services in one booking" in the calendar settings (`multi_service_booking` in `PUT /api/v1/calendar/settings`; off by default). Customers then add more services on the booking page, see the total time and price, and choose anyone, one person for all, or a different person for each. Staff do the same from New booking, and a booking's details list each service with its time and person. Each person is busy only for their own services; moving or cancelling acts on the whole booking (WHIT-1173).
