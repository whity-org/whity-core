- **The database itself now refuses double bookings.** Every live booking's people and rooms are held in the database, and PostgreSQL refuses two bookings that overlap for the same person or room, whatever wrote them. Book anyway still lets staff double-book a person on purpose, never a room. Bookings that already overlapped are kept as they are and listed by `whity-cli calendar:overlaps`. A booking sent twice from the public page, for example when a slow network retries it, is now booked once (WHIT-1412).
