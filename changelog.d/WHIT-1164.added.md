- **Form printouts read the way a committee's paper does.** Questions that
  share a print group (an applicant's name, ID, email and phone) print as one
  numbered block; a checklist of Yes / No / N/A questions prints as one list
  with its files folded in as "Attachment 1, 2 …"; a signature slot nobody had
  to sign is left out; question numbers with a letter ("1.2a") are kept; a
  table's choice column prints its label. "For official use" shows one block
  per stage (faculty committee, dean, IRB) with what was entered, by whom and
  when, and a stage's decision picked from a list, such as "Conditionally
  approved", is the decision the printout carries. A long answer's "Most
  words" limit, offered in the question panel, is now kept and checked
  (WHIT-1164).
