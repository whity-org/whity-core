- **Sign in only with the identity provider.** A new server setting, Password
  sign-in (`auth.password_sign_in`, on by default), lets an operator sign
  everyone in and up through the server's identity provider, as app.whity.dev
  does with Whity ID. Off, the sign-in and sign-up pages show only the
  provider, password sign-in, reset and two-step set-up stop, and invitations
  are accepted by signing in. Existing sessions and tokens keep working.
  `whity-cli auth password-sign-in on` or `WHITY_PASSWORD_SIGN_IN=force` turns
  it back on if the provider is down (WHIT-1168).
