- **Staff and time for appointments.** Each person who provides a service can have their own length, time kept free after and price; the booking keeps what it used, also when it moves, and the booking page shows the person's values, or a range ("30–45 min") for anyone. A branch can have its own time zone (Calendar › Settings › Branch time zones): the hours and bookings of the people who work there use it, a group's room or chair is taken from their branch, and the booking page, emails and texts write times in it. Saving time off over bookings lists them, each with Move (the customer is asked first) or Keep them. When a Google or Microsoft calendar can't be read, booking stays open and the calendar shows "Couldn't check Amro Saleh's calendar in Google Calendar at 09:14" to the desk and the person; these failures are kept for a week and counted on the status page under External calendars. Free times are tested across every clock change of a year in six time zones, Lord Howe's half-hour change included. New routes: `GET /api/v1/calendar/branches`, `PATCH /api/v1/calendar/branches/{id}` and `GET /api/v1/calendar/busy-checks`, each an MCP tool (WHIT-1440).
