- **Workspace handles.** A workspace's address name is now its @handle, unique on the installation: Latin letters, numbers and dashes, 3 to 30 characters, never one of Whity's own page or system words. Only an owner changes it, once every 30 days, and the old handle keeps working for 90 days, so links already shared don't break. Arabic workspace names now get a suggested Latin handle instead of being refused. Operators can keep extra handles from use (Handles nobody may take), and `whity-cli handles:check` shows what the upgrade does to each workspace's handle before it runs. Plugins hear about a change through `tenant.handle_changed` (SDK 1.159) (WHIT-1363, design section 36).
