- **Platform operators can't be locked out when only Whity ID signs people in.** Turning password sign-in off now refuses while any member of the system workspace has no Whity ID linked, and names them; `whity-cli auth password-sign-in status` lists them, and `--force` turns it off anyway with a plain warning. An operator who can't sign in gets a one-time link from the server with `whity-cli auth operator-link <email>`: it works once, for 15 minutes, only for that operator, and every step is in the audit log. In Settings › Security, anyone signed in can now link Whity ID even when its email differs from their account's.
