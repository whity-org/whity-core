- **Batches, serial numbers and expiry dates in inventory.** An item can keep batches (each with its expiry date) or a serial number per piece. Stock coming in names its batch, and a new code makes it, with its expiry from the date typed or the item's shelf life. Stock leaving takes the batch that expires first, unless a batch is named; a workspace can choose oldest first instead. A transfer keeps its batches on the way, and a count names the batch any extra stock belongs to. Turning batches on for an item that already has stock puts that stock into a batch called OPENING. Each day, batches expiring within the warning window (7 days unless changed) and batches that have expired are reported once each, to plugins and the Stock assistant. New API routes list an item's batches and what is expiring, and plugins read them through the SDK (1.134) (WHIT-1235).
