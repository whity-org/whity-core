- **Owners can remove a co-owner, or stop being one.** In Settings › Owners an owner removes another owner, or steps down themselves, after confirming it's them. A workspace always keeps at least one owner. The person keeps their roles there and is told by notification and email. Offers they made can no longer be accepted. People shows an "Owner" mark, and an owner can't be removed from the workspace in People until they stop being an owner (WHIT-1171).
