- **A workspace's company details have their own settings.** `company.name`, `company.address`, `company.tax_id`, `company.phone` and `company.email` replace `billing.seller_*`; a migration moves existing values once. Documents, letterheads and invoices read them, so a document theme can now print the phone and email on the letterhead. Workspaces can set them under Settings, General, and the platform defaults gain a Company section. Issued invoices keep the seller they were issued with (WHIT-788).
