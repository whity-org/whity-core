- **Comments and suggestions in the document editor (editor M5).** The editor has three modes: Editing, Suggesting, where your edits become suggestions someone accepts, and Viewing. People who can read a document but not edit it work in Suggesting (E.10). Suggested text shows dashed in the main colour, and suggested deletions stay visible, struck through in the error colour. Anyone who can edit accepts or rejects a suggestion, one at a time or all at once (or all from one agent), and every decision is in the audit log with who made it (E.29). Comments mark their text on screen only, never in print (E.39). They have replies, @mentions that notify the person, resolve and reopen. They sit in a margin rail beside the text, or a drawer on narrow screens (E.6). Open suggestions block publishing a template (E.37) (WHIT-726).
- **Two new permissions, `documents:suggest` and `documents:comment`.** Migration 186 grants them to every role that reads documents. A suggestion is stored as a patch anchored to block ids and text, never written into the draft. Accepting it applies it to the draft and bumps the draft's version. A draft save that removes a suggestion's text marks it stale. Agents can suggest and comment, recorded as the agent acting for its person, but never decide. New routes under `/api/v1/document-sources/{id}`: `suggestions`, `suggestions/{sid}/decision`, `suggestions/decisions`, `comments` (with messages, resolve and reopen), `mentionable` and `changes` (WHIT-726).
