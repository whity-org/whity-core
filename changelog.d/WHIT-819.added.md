- **Connect an AI client as one of your workspace agents.** Connecting to `/mcp/agents/<slug>` signs the client in as that agent, acting for you, with your grant for it: writes are Ask first. It is its own OAuth resource with its own metadata, and it works once you use that agent in the workspace (`POST /api/me/agents/{id}/assignment`); before that, the answer says so. Only that agent can use the address: a person's own token, or a credential of another agent, is refused there. An agent's credential from `/api/me/agents/{id}/credentials` works there too (WHIT-819).
