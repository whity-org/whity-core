- **Appointment reports: no-show and late-cancel rates.** Calendar › Reports shows, for this week, this month, last month, the last 3, 6 or 12 months or chosen dates, how many bookings were due, the no-show, late-cancel and missed rates with how they changed since the period before, and how many bookings nobody marked (left out of the rates, with a note when that is more than one in ten). It breaks them down by staff member, service, customer, day and hour, how they were booked, reminder, or new and returning customers, and downloads as a spreadsheet in English or Arabic. People with `calendar:reports` see everyone; everyone else sees their own. The home has a new Appointments card with this month's rates and the customers who missed most. `GET /api/v1/calendar/attendance/report` serves the report, and agents can read it as an MCP tool (WHIT-1437).
