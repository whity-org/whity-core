- **Plugins can issue documents from their jobs, where a workspace allows it (Plugin SDK 1.58).** A plugin that declares `issuesInBackground()` may issue through `Documents` from its jobs with no person, but only in workspaces whose admins allowed it, only from its own slots, and with only the permissions it declares. `GET /api/document-background-issuing` lists such plugins with their slots, and `PUT /api/document-background-issuing/{plugin}` allows or stops one (`documents:publish`; agents can only propose it). The allow-list is the tenant setting `documents.background_issuing`, which the settings PATCH refuses. Such a document is recorded with no person, the system is the audit actor, and the document events say `origin: plugin:<name>`. Otherwise the refusal is `background_not_allowed` (WHIT-742).
