- **Printed documents use the document faces.** Whity Render now embeds Readex Pro 600 for document titles and Geist Mono for codes (barcode text, QR references), the app's own font files, so a PDF's title matches the editor instead of falling back to Noto Sans. The render image copies them from `packages/tokens/fonts`, and the render-tier CI job checks the PDF embeds both (WHIT-733).
