- **Plugins add tabs to the customer area.** With SDK 1.173 a plugin can give a workspace's customers their own tab, Home card or page at `/@handle`, built from blocks or an embedded page: a learning plugin's courses, a clinic's treatment plans. Core keeps each customer to their own records: the area calls the plugin with a short-lived token for that workspace that opens only the plugin's routes declared for customers, and tells the plugin who the customer is. On a phone, tabs past five move under More (WHIT-1424).
