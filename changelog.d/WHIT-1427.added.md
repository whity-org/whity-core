- **Did they come: no-shows and late cancels are recorded.** Checking a customer in marks that they came; once a booking has started, Didn't come marks it, and Mark as came changes it back, each with who and when, audited. The calendar's new Did they come? card lists today's bookings nobody has marked, 15 minutes after their start. Cancelling asks whether the customer asked (early or late by the workspace's window, 24 hours by default) or we cancelled, which never counts. A customer who missed 2 bookings in the last 12 months shows "Missed 2 of the last 5" on their bookings, and their record says "Came 12 · Didn't come 2 · Late cancels 1 · since March 2026"; someone who manages the calendar can start their count again. Marking at the end of the day is a switch, off by default. `GET /api/v1/calendar/attendance` gives the rates (with the new `calendar:reports` permission, or your own), and `calendar.attendance.marked` is a new event (SDK 1.175) (WHIT-1427).
