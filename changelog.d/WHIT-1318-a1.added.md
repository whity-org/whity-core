- **Accounting starts: the chart of accounts and a double-entry ledger that is never edited.** Opening accounting gives a workspace its books: a chart of accounts with plain names in English and Arabic and the accountant's codes beside them (the Jordan chart is the default), seven journals, and an Accountant role for a company's outside accountant. Journal entries are drafted, then posted; a posted entry is never changed, only reversed by its mirror, and each one is chained to the one before by a hash, so `whity-cli accounting:verify-chain` shows whether anything was edited behind the books' back. Months can be closed and reopened with a reason, and the trial balance and each account's statement read from monthly balances that `whity-cli accounting:rebuild-balances` can recompute. Everything is under `/api/accounting`, in the `core.accounting` MCP toolset (agents only propose), behind the new `accounting:read`, `accounting:post`, `accounting:close` and `accounting:settings` permissions, and in the plugin SDK as `$ctx->accounting()` (SDK 1.131). Invoices, bills, payments and the sales tax return follow in the next phases (WHIT-1318).
