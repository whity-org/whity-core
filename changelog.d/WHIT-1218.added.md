- **An approval certificate opens an approved form's final document (WHIT-1218).** When a route approves a form response that prints in the automatic layout, its PDF now starts with a one-page certificate: the workspace letterhead with its logo, a title such as "Certificate of Ethics Approval", a sentence certifying the decision, the project title (the answer, not the form's name), the reference, decided date and conditions, the people the route template names, the approvals, and the verification QR when the workspace prints QR codes. The application printout follows unchanged and keeps its own page numbers. Set the title, who certifies, the subject question and up to four people rows on the route template's final document stage (`rule_config.certificate`); every option has a default. English and Arabic. Whity Render takes `coverPages` for a cover that carries no running header or footer and is not counted in the page numbers, and refuses a cover longer than its pages.
