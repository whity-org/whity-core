- whity.dev reads its plans from Whity: `GET /api/public/plans` answers the website's plans (WS.5) from the active plans, their monthly prices and limits, plus a small marketing layer in the new **Plans on the website** setting (Arabic name, tagline, features shown, highlighted plan, button). Prices are in normal units, only plans the page can show are sent, and the answer is cached for five minutes.
- The website's contact form is answered by Whity: `POST /api/public/contact` files each message in the Messages inbox of the workspace named in the new **Website contact form inbox** setting, by email when the sender gave an address. It is limited per address, drops anything that fills the hidden trap field, never sends the message back, and works without scripts (the page gets a redirect back). Both endpoints let `https://whity.dev` read them (`WHITY_SITE_ORIGINS` to change it).
