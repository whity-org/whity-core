- **Plugins can put leave on the calendar and ask who is away (WHIT-1210, #2132).** SDK 1.120 adds two host services. `Whity\Sdk\Calendar\TimeOff` creates, cancels and lists a person's time off on core's calendar as the acting person, with the time-off route's checks (`calendar:write` for one's own, `calendar:manage` for anybody else's); it is real time off, so the person is away in bookings, team views and "who's in". A plugin's own reference (`keyhub.leave:123`) makes creating it idempotent, backed by a unique index (migration 307), and refusals throw `Calendar\TimeOffRejected` with a stable reason. `Whity\Sdk\Delegation\Away::at()` answers whether a person is away at a moment, from their "I'm away" period or their time off, and who stands in for them. "I'm away" gains a fourth thing to hand over, Approvals in plugins, so a plugin's own approvals can follow the stand-in. English and Arabic.
