- **Treatment plans.** A customer can have a plan: the person who treats them and a series of visits, each a service and a gap after the one before ("14 to 21 days"). Staff make one on the customer's record, from a service's ready-made plan ("Laser, 6 sessions") or by hand. Book the next visit finds the first free time in the gap with the same person, and books it when staff say so. Book all books every visit still to book, each checked like any booking; a visit that can't be placed is left unbooked and said so. Progress ("3 of 6 done") is read from the bookings, and a visit the customer didn't come to shows as missed. The booking shows which visit of the plan it is. The customer's name and email are always read from their contact and never copied onto the plan, so deleting the contact deletes their plans. New routes under `/api/v1/calendar/plans`, `GET /api/v1/calendar/bookings/{id}/plan`, and `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/calendar/services/{id}/plan-templates`, each an MCP tool (WHIT-1444).
