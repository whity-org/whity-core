- **People signed in to Whity find their name and email already filled on a workspace's booking page.** The page fills them from the person's own Whity account, says "Filled in from your Whity account. You can change it.", and books with whatever they send. Nothing from any workspace is used, and nothing reaches the workspace until they book. The details come from a new `GET /api/v1/public/booking/{workspace}/me`, which is never cached (`private, no-store`) and answers `null` for a signed-out visitor. Phone stays empty until a Whity account carries a phone number (WHIT-1179).
