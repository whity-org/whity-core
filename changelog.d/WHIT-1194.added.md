- **Workspace templates (SDK 1.116).** A plugin can offer ready starts for one kind of business (a salon, a clinic, a bakery) through `PluginWorkspaceTemplatesInterface`. Each template is a versioned manifest, data only: workspace defaults, booking rules and hours, services priced per currency, roles, forms, documents, example contacts and bookings, its guided setup steps, and the paid plugin it offers with a trial. `GET /api/workspace/templates` lists them with core's Blank, `POST /api/workspace/templates/{key}/apply` applies one as the caller, adding only what is missing and granting a role only permissions the caller holds, and `DELETE /api/workspace/templates/examples` removes the examples in one click. Templates are free. Migration 305 records which template a workspace chose and what it made.
