While a paid plugin is on in a workspace (bought, paid in cash, on its free trial or in grace), the number limits on its add-on plan apply, as if the add-on were held: Whity for Salons lifts the staff limit for as long as the salon has it. Invitations now ask the plan's staff limit first, as `seats.enforcement` says: `block` refuses with a message pointing to a larger plan, `warn` sends the invitation and says so. Nobody is ever removed.
