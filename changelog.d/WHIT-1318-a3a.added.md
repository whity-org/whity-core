- **Supplier bills, payments to suppliers and salon commission in the books.** A workspace enters its suppliers' bills with their own invoice numbers, lines for costs or things bought, and the sales tax it can reclaim from each line's tax category; posting a bill numbers it (`BIL-2026-0001`), freezes it and books the costs and the tax to reclaim against what is owed to the supplier, after the workspace's bill approval route when one is chosen. A supplier credit (`SCR-2026-0001`) reverses all or part of a bill. Money paid to a supplier, by cash, transfer, CliQ, card or cheque, posts at once and settles the bills named; a cheque dated later waits on cheques we wrote until it clears. Aged payables and a supplier's statement show what is owed to whom. Closing a salon's commission period now books staff commission against commission to pay, one line per person, from two new accounts the chart gains. Everything is under `/api/accounting/bills`, `/payments-made`, `/reports/aged-payables` and `/suppliers/{id}/statement`, in the `core.accounting` MCP toolset (agents only propose), behind the new `bills:read` and `bills:write` permissions (the Accountant role gets both), and in the plugin SDK through `invoices()` with `kind => purchase` and `payments()->recordMade()` (SDK 1.157, WHIT-1318).
