- **The waitlist.** Customers who find no time that suits them can join the waitlist on the booking page, choosing the days and times of day (mornings, afternoons, evenings or any), and the desk can add someone under Calendar › Waitlist. When a cancel, a move or "I can't come" frees a time, everyone waiting who matches it (the same service, that person or anyone, the day and the time of day) is told at once by email, and by WhatsApp or SMS when connected, with a link to a page with that one time. The first to take it gets it: taking it is an ordinary booking, and the database's guarantee against double bookings decides who was first, so the others see "Someone took this time first" and stay on the waitlist. An offer ends at the time's start minus the minimum notice. Staff can also offer a free time by hand, or take someone off the list, and the front desk shows how many are waiting. New routes: `GET` and `POST /api/v1/calendar/waitlist`, `POST /api/v1/calendar/waitlist/{id}/remove` and `/offer` (each an MCP tool), and the public `POST /api/v1/public/booking/{workspace}/waitlist`, `GET /api/v1/public/waitlist-offers/{token}` and its `/accept` (WHIT-1442).
