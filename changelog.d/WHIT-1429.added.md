- **See what a workspace template adds before you choose it, and take its updates when you want them.** Choosing a template now shows, item by item, what it will add, what is already there, and anything it can't add and why. When a template your workspace uses gets a new version, the dashboard and Settings › General say so. "See what's new" lists the new pieces, which are added only when you apply the update. For each item the template has changed, "Use the template's version" updates that one item, without deleting anything (WHIT-1429).
