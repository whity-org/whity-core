- **Intake forms and the visit record.** A service can ask customers to fill forms (section 22) on booking or before the visit, for their first visit or every one, required or not. The booking email and the page after booking link to each form, filled in with the name, email and phone they gave, and the answer is kept with the booking and the customer. The desk and the booking show whether the form is done, with Send again and Fill in at the desk (the form on a tablet); a missing form never stops check-in. Each booking has a visit record: the notes of the person treating the customer, the forms sent for it with their printouts, and files kept in the workspace's own storage. Only the treating staff member and people granted the new `calendar:visit_notes` permission see it (no role has it until an admin grants it, and an agent needs it too); customers never do. Finish visit signs the notes, and after that they never change: only dated corrections are added. Every read and change is written to the audit log. Not a medical records system; stored in the EU. New routes: `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/calendar/services/{id}/forms`, `POST /api/v1/calendar/bookings/{id}/intake/{formId}`, and the visit record under `/api/v1/calendar/bookings/{id}/visit` and `/api/v1/calendar/visit-files/{id}`, each an MCP tool (WHIT-1443).
