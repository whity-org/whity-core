- **A bracketed note after Latin or numbers prints the right way round in Arabic documents.** In "10:05 (بتوقيت عمّان)", "CS 101 (مختبر)" or "Ali (علي)", the opening bracket was kept inside the left-to-right part and could print mirrored or apart from its closing bracket. It now stays with the note, and the same holds for an Arabic run followed by a bracketed Latin note in an English document (#1915, WHIT-1213).
