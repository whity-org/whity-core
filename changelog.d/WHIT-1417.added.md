- **Booking reminders always go out, twice by default.** Each booking now gets a reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before, by email and by WhatsApp or SMS when connected; a workspace that chose its own hours keeps them, and Calendar settings take up to three. If the queue loses a reminder, a sweep every 5 minutes sends it, and never twice. The booking shows the desk each reminder sent, or why one failed ("Reminder failed: no phone"). The reminder asks "Are you coming?": I'm coming and I can't come open the customer's booking page, which asks for the press and tells the desk (WHIT-1417).
