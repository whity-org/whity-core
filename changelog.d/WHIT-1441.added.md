- **The front desk.** Calendar › Front desk shows today's bookings for people with `calendar:front_desk`, as a column for each staff member (or each room or piece of equipment) with now as a red line, or as a list. Each booking moves through Expected, Arrived, With staff, Done or Didn't come, one labelled button at a time (or the keys I, S, F and N on the selected booking), and each step can be undone. The side panel shows who is waiting now, with minutes waited, walk-ins included, and Did they come?. A customer running late is marked with minutes and nothing moves; a staff member running late shows the rest of their day pushed back, marks what can't move as needing a decision, and Move and tell them moves the rest and tells each customer the new time. The main button adds a walk-in when walk-ins are on, otherwise a booking. The day loads in one request and checks for changes every 20 seconds. On a phone it is a list with Check in on each row and the rest in a sheet. New routes: `GET /api/v1/calendar/desk`, `POST /api/v1/calendar/bookings/{id}/desk-status` and `/late`, and `GET` and `POST /api/v1/calendar/staff/{profileId}/late`, each an MCP tool (WHIT-1441).
