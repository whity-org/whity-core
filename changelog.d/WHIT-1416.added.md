- **The customer area.** A workspace's customers sign in at `/@handle` and see their own bookings (move and cancel them), quotes (accept or decline), invoices with what is left to pay and how to pay it, receipts, and their messages with the business, in the workspace's logo and colour, in English and Arabic, on a computer or a phone. Past bookings and bills appear by themselves when the customer's verified email matches the business's contact, and never by an unverified one. Settings › Customer area turns it on or off, hides tabs and sets the welcome line and How to pay. Design section 41, CA.1–CA.24 (WHIT-1416).
