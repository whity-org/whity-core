- **Workspaces have owners, and a signed-in person can create more workspaces.** Whoever creates a workspace now owns it (sign-up, Whity ID, the operator, or the new "Create workspace" at the foot of the workspace switcher and in Ctrl+K `/new workspace`). Existing workspaces get their oldest admin as owner (migration 300). Only owners pay invoices and change the plan; every other admin power is unchanged. Operators switch creating on with `workspaces.self_create_enabled` and cap it with `workspaces.max_owned_per_person` (0 = no limit) on the Sign-up tab. A new workspace starts on the default plan, behind the payment gate when payment is required, and no password is asked for (WHIT-1169).
