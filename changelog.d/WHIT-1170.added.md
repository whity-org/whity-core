- **Owners can hand a workspace over, or add co-owners.** Settings › Owners lists who owns the workspace. An owner offers ownership ("Hand over ownership") or co-ownership ("Add a co-owner") to an active member. The person accepts or declines within 7 days, and both confirm it's them first. Nothing changes until it is accepted. After a hand-over the old owner stays an admin, unless they chose to leave the workspace. Both people get an in-app notification and an email in the workspace's language, and every step is in the audit log. Co-owners are equal owners. Migration 301; SDK 1.112 adds `workspace.owner_added`, `workspace.owner_removed` and `workspace.ownership_transferred` (WHIT-1170, WHIT-1171).
