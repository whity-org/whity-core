- **Workspace templates can set up review routes.** A template can now bring a review route in Flows for one of its forms, such as "reception checks, then a doctor approves". Each step is for the people in one of the template's roles. Applying the template makes the route and sends the form's answers through it, unless the form already has a route of its own. You need to be allowed to design review routes; otherwise the route is left out and everything else is still set up. Plugin SDK 1.172 (WHIT-1419).
