- **A payment step when creating a further workspace (WHIT-1207).** After creating a workspace that starts a paid trial (WHIT-1203), its owner is asked for a card at `/setup/pay`: the card is taken now, the trial stays free, and the first charge is when it ends. "Not now" keeps the trial; the workspace becomes read-only when it ends unpaid. The step appears only when a payment provider is set up that can start a trial, and the first, free workspace never sees it. A banner across the app tells everyone when a workspace is read-only, with Pay for people who can pay (others are told to ask an owner), and reminds payers from 7 days before a trial ends. New `GET /api/v1/billing/trial`; `POST /api/v1/billing/checkout` keeps the rest of a running trial free for the workspace's own plan and takes `after: "setup"`. SDK 1.118 adds `Whity\Sdk\Billing\TrialCheckout` for billing plugins whose provider can start a subscription with a trial. English and Arabic.
